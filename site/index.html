Repairs, tile & resurfacing

Everything beyond the weekly clean, from equipment swaps to a brand-new finish.

  • Pool Pump Replacement

    Loud, leaking or dead pump? We replace it, often with an energy-saving variable-speed model.

  • Pool Filter Replacement & Cleaning

    Cartridge, D.E. and sand filters: cleaned, rebuilt or replaced so the water stays clear.

  • Pool & Spa Heater Repair

    Heater won't fire or won't hold temperature? We troubleshoot gas heaters and heat pumps.

  • Pool Tile Cleaning

    White calcium lines and scale removed from the waterline so your tile looks new again.

  • Pool Tile Repair

    Loose, cracked or missing waterline tile reset and replaced before water gets behind it.

  • Pool Acid Wash

    Drain-and-clean treatment that removes stains and a thin layer of plaster to brighten the finish.

  • Pool Replastering & Resurfacing

    Rough, etched or stained plaster replaced with a smooth new finish that looks and feels like new.
