Pool filter replacement & cleaning

A clogged or broken filter means cloudy water, a struggling pump and more chemicals. We clean, rebuild and replace cartridge, D.E. and sand filters.

Get a free quote (818) 857-1463

Signs your filter needs attention

  • Pressure gauge reading well above its clean starting pressure
  • Cloudy water that won't clear even with balanced chemistry
  • D.E. or sand blowing back into the pool
  • Torn, flattened or crumbling cartridges
  • Cracked tank, leaking band clamp or a broken gauge

What we do

  • Filter cleaning: cartridges hosed and degreased, D.E. grids broken down and cleaned, sand filters backwashed
  • Cartridge and grid replacement when the media is worn out
  • Full filter replacement when the tank is cracked or the filter is undersized
  • O-rings, gauges and air relief valves replaced as needed

How often should a filter be cleaned?

Most residential filters need a full cleaning a few times a year, and more often after a green-to-clean or heavy storms. If you're on weekly service, we watch the pressure for you and tell you when it's time.

Common questions

How often should pool filter cartridges be replaced?

It depends on use and how often they're cleaned, but cartridges commonly last a few years. When they're torn, flattened or no longer clean up, it's time.

Which filter type is best?

Cartridge filters are common and easy to maintain, D.E. filters filter the finest particles, and sand filters are simple and durable. We'll talk through what fits your pool.

Why is my filter pressure high?

Usually the filter is dirty. If pressure is still high right after cleaning, there may be a restriction in the plumbing or a problem with the media.
