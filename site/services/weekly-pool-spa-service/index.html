Weekly pool & spa service

A clean, balanced pool every week without you lifting a skimmer. We show up on the same day, take care of the water and the equipment, and tell you if anything needs attention.

Get a free quote (818) 857-1463

What every weekly visit includes

  • Test the water and balance chlorine, pH and alkalinity
  • Skim the surface and empty skimmer and pump baskets
  • Brush the walls, steps and waterline
  • Vacuum the floor as needed
  • Check the pump, filter, heater and timer for leaks, noise or pressure changes
  • Service the attached spa or stand-alone hot tub at the same visit
  • Let you know about anything that needs a repair before it becomes expensive

Our show-up guarantee

Most people switch pool companies for one reason: the pool guy stopped showing up. So we put it in writing. We'll never miss your scheduled weekly service, or your whole month is free.

Same day every week, rain or shine. If something ever forces a change, we'll tell you ahead of time and make it up.

Why weekly service pays for itself

Valley heat, Santa Ana winds and summer storms swing pool chemistry fast. A pool that is checked every week stays ahead of algae and scale, which means fewer green-pool emergencies, longer-lasting plaster and equipment that doesn't have to work as hard.

Catching a small leak at the pump seal or a filter that is running high pressure early is almost always cheaper than replacing the part later.

Pools and spas

Hot tubs and attached spas run hotter and see heavier use per gallon than a pool, so their water needs closer attention. We include spa care in your route, whether it shares the pool's equipment or has its own.

How getting started works

  1. Request a quote. Call, text or send the form with your address and pool size.
  2. First visit. We look at the pool, spa and equipment and go over what it needs.
  3. Your weekly day. You're added to a regular route on a set day each week.

Common questions

How much does weekly pool service cost?

Pricing depends on the size of the pool, whether there's a spa, and how much debris the yard drops in. Call, text or send the form for a free quote for your address.

We'll never miss your scheduled weekly service. If we do, you don't pay for that month.

Do I need to be home for weekly service?

No. As long as we can reach the gate and the equipment, we can service the pool while you're out.

Ask us when you request a quote and we'll spell out exactly what's included for your pool.

Do you service spas and hot tubs on their own?

Yes. We service attached spas along with the pool, and stand-alone hot tubs as well.
