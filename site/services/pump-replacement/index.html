Pool pump replacement

The pump keeps your water moving through the filter, and when it fails, the pool goes downhill fast. We repair what can be repaired and replace what can't, sized correctly for your pool.

Get a free quote (818) 857-1463

Signs your pump is failing

  • Loud grinding or screeching (usually worn motor bearings)
  • Humming but not starting
  • Water leaking from the pump or under the motor
  • Trouble priming or air in the pump basket
  • Tripping the breaker
  • Weak circulation even with clean baskets and filter

Variable-speed pumps

Older single-speed pumps run at full power all the time. A variable-speed pump can run slower for longer, which moves the same water while using much less electricity. In California, replacement pool pumps generally need to meet current energy efficiency rules, so most replacements today are variable-speed.

We'll recommend a pump that fits your pool size, plumbing and equipment, not just the biggest one available.

What's included

  1. Diagnosis. Confirm whether the pump, the motor or something else is the real problem.
  2. Options and quote. Repair versus replacement, with prices for each where both make sense.
  3. Installation. Remove the old pump, plumb and wire the new one, check unions and seals.
  4. Startup. Prime, set speeds and schedule, and confirm good flow through the filter.

Common questions

How long do pool pumps last?

Many pumps last around 8 to 12 years, depending on use, maintenance and conditions at the equipment pad. Motors and seals often fail before the pump housing.

Is a variable-speed pump worth it?

For most pools, yes. They're quieter and typically use much less electricity than older single-speed pumps.

Can you just replace the motor?

Sometimes. If the housing and impeller are in good shape, a motor replacement can make sense. We'll tell you which option is better value.
