Green to clean pool recovery

A green pool isn't a lost cause. We kill the algae, clean out the debris and filter, rebalance the water and bring it back to clear, then keep it that way with weekly service if you'd like.

Get a free quote (818) 857-1463

When you need a green-to-clean

  • The water is green, cloudy or you can't see the floor
  • The pool sat while a house was empty, for sale or being remodeled
  • The pump or filter was down for a while
  • A storm or heat wave pushed chemistry out of balance
  • You just bought a home with a neglected pool

How we bring it back

  1. Remove debris. Leaves and sludge come out first so chemicals go to work on algae, not dirt.
  2. Shock and treat. We dose the water to kill algae and break down organic material.
  3. Brush everything. Walls, floor, steps and corners, to knock algae loose.
  4. Filter and clean the filter. The filter works hard during a recovery, so it gets cleaned as dead algae is pulled out.
  5. Vacuum and rebalance. Once the water clears, we vacuum what has settled and balance the chemistry.
  6. Follow-up visits. We return until the water is clear and stable.

How long does it take?

Most pools clear within a few visits, but timing depends on how far gone the water is, the size of the pool and how well the filter and pump are working. If equipment is part of the problem, we'll tell you up front.

Selling a home? A clear pool photographs better and avoids a red flag at inspection. Ask about getting the pool ready before listing photos.

Common questions

Do you need to drain a green pool?

Usually not. Most green pools can be cleared with treatment, brushing and filtration. Draining is only needed in specific cases, like very high dissolved solids or when an acid wash is planned.

Why did my pool turn green so fast?

Algae grows quickly when chlorine drops, especially in summer heat. A failed pump, a skipped week or a heavy storm is often all it takes.

Is a green pool dangerous?

It's not safe to swim in, and stagnant water can attract mosquitoes. It's worth fixing quickly.
